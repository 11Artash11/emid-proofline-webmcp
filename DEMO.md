# Demo plan — WebMCP Challenge 2026

Target length: 2 minutes 35 seconds. Record the live app and real site-tool calls
with narration. Do not use private EMID records, correspondence, or existing
private video footage.

## Storyboard

### 0:00–0:20 — The problem

Open on the Project Horizon case status.

> Intelligence is only useful when you can verify what it is saying. Proofline
> gives a person and an agent the same evidence-aware workspace.

### 0:20–0:50 — Trace a claim

Ask the agent:

> Use the site tools to trace CLM-003 to its sources. Is funding approval
> documented?

Show the trace view selecting CLM-003. The expected result is one declared call
note and an explicit boundary: likelihood is not a decision notice.

### 0:50–1:20 — Find what is missing

Ask:

> List the critical evidence gaps and tell me exactly what would close them.

Show GAP-001 and GAP-002. Emphasize that missing evidence remains visible rather
than being filled with a guess.

### 1:20–1:50 — Move from analysis to a safe next step

Ask:

> Propose the next steps for status and timing.

Show the ranked plan and its literal boundary: no message, filing, status change,
upload, or external commitment has occurred.

### 1:50–2:20 — Human approval

Ask:

> Prepare a proposal to request a completeness confirmation for GAP-001.

Show the proposal appearing in the human review queue. Approve it in the demo UI
and show that its state becomes “Approved as proposal · not executed.”

### 2:20–2:35 — Close

> EMID Proofline: trace claims, expose gaps, and keep humans in control. Integrity
> before intelligence.

## Expected tools

1. `get_case_status`
2. `trace_claim_to_sources`
3. `list_evidence_gaps`
4. `propose_next_steps`
5. `prepare_case_change`

- Use only Project Horizon.
- Keep every browser tab, notification, account name, and local path outside the
  frame.
- Confirm the final recording is under three minutes and includes audio.
- Review the exact export before uploading. Upload and submission require a new,
  explicit approval from Artashes Nazaryan.
